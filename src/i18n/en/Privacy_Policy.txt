## **Information about General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**

### **What is GDPR?**

The General Data Protection Regulation is an EU data protection law that regulates how organizations collect, use, store, share, and protect the personal information of individuals in the EEA.

### **What is personal data?**

Personal data is any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

It may include:

- Names.
- Email addresses.
- IP addresses.
- Device identifiers.
- Billing information.
- Account information.
- Online activity.
- Location information.
- Other information that can identify a person directly or indirectly.

### **Legal bases for processing**

Where GDPR applies, we process personal information under one or more of the following legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract:** Processing is necessary to provide the Service, process a purchase, deliver access to the game, or respond to a request.
- **Legitimate interests:** Processing is necessary for reasonable business interests, such as improving the Service, preventing fraud, securing our systems, and providing customer support, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.
- **Consent:** You have consented to a particular type of processing, such as certain analytics, marketing communications, or advertising cookies.
- **Legal obligation:** Processing is necessary to comply with tax, accounting, regulatory, fraud prevention, or other legal requirements.

### **Data protection principles**

Where GDPR applies, personal information must be:

- Processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently.
- Collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes.
- Limited to information necessary for those purposes.
- Accurate and kept up to date where necessary.
- Retained no longer than necessary.
- Protected using appropriate security measures.
- Processed in a manner that allows organizations to demonstrate compliance.
